# Bhavishya Tarun

I like taking messy technical problems seriously, then making them clear enough for people to build on.

I’m a software engineer who enjoys the stretch between an idea and a dependable implementation. I like learning unfamiliar systems, finding the constraint that actually matters, and making the result useful to the next person who has to understand or maintain it.

## How I work

- I move between backend details and the larger question of how a tool should help someone.
- I care about evidence: what happened, why a decision was made, and how we know it worked.
- I like good abstractions, but I test them against real data, real interfaces, and real maintenance.
- I write handoffs and demos so another engineer can inspect a subsystem without needing the whole story retold.
- I’m interested in applied AI when it improves a workflow, clarifies a decision, or helps people reason.
